Profile

Enterprise change programmes touch finance, operations and IT at the same time, and stall the moment those three stop talking to each other. That gap is where my work sits. Ten years in, the path has run from project support and analysis, through delivery leadership, to owning programmes end to end: scope, plan, partners, and the people who have to use the system on Monday morning.

Currently Senior Project Manager at a fintech listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, leading its enterprise-wide Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations implementation. Before that, nearly eight years at a FTSE 250 food manufacturer in the UK, delivering IT and ERP change across two dozen manufacturing sites.
